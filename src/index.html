What this is

16 Smarsh Enterprise Conduct repositories on a single factory floor. One communication rides a conveyor belt through the surveillance data path — ec-gateway, ec-queue-qualifier, ec-surveillance-filter, ec-surveillance-policy-evaluator, ec-surveillance-quota-manager, ec-alerting-service, ec-echo-engine, ec-indexer — while six more services stand off the line as the machinery that feeds it, and two more keep its record.

ec-centralised-audit and ec-reporting are deliberately not on the belt: they consume events about the communication, so the communication never travels to them. They stand inside the loop the belt makes, and the audit tower is fed by trenches cut under the floor. Four forks leave the line early — nothing claims it, nothing qualifies, every content verdict ages out, not sampled — and three of them end the journey where they happen.

How much of it is real

Genuinely computed, live, in your browser: the whole ranged-GET plan — chunk count, concurrency, waves and bytes on the wire from the document size you choose; Kafka consumer lag and the KEDA replica count it drives; retry-ladder timing; the quota counter; the pipeline fan-out; the Elasticsearch bulk flush; echo detection; and end-to-end latency. Move a slider and the machinery moves because the arithmetic changed, not because an animation was swapped.

Assumed: S3 first-byte 25 ms, throughput 60 KB/ms, minification 0.12, Mongo R/W 4/8 ms, Redis INCR 1 ms, ES bulk 40 ms + 200 KB/ms, policy evaluation 0.5 ms, 80% of a document's participants in the monitored population, a flag policy matching on 75% of the pipelines an ignore policy did not suppress, four policies per pipeline, and 90% of surveilled communications passing the sampling profile's participant filters.

Controlled, not assumed: the Cognition round trip. It defaults to 45 000 ms, but it is the one latency EC's own code does not bound — so it is a slider, and it reaches past the 9 000 000 ms COMS ceiling on purpose.

Scaled: the Cognition wait (a 9 000 000 ms ceiling shown in seconds) and every machine's work cycle — ec-gateway's press takes about 2.5 s a stroke to do what the model prices at roughly 200 ms. The figures on its readout are the computed ones.

Faked: floor shapes, belt texture, machine livery, and the two opaque identifiers on display — the window token and the echo fingerprint are plausible shapes, not real values.
