## Terminology — read this first

This repo began life as "EC City Tour", an isometric **city** with service
**buildings** and a **road network** (see commit `36721c4`). It was later converted
to a **factory**: a conveyor belt, machines, a raised belt deck, hazard banding.

**Nothing on this floor is a building any more. Everything is machinery.** When I
say "building" in this or any future prompt, I mean **a machine on the factory
floor** — read it that way and never revert to city idiom. Concretely that means:

- No gabled or pitched roofs, no roof slabs with overhangs.
- No lit domestic windows. Machines get louvres, vents, sight glasses, inspection
  ports, status lamps, gauges, hoppers, ducts, cable entries.
- No house/hall proportions. Machines read as chunky industrial casings on a
  plinth, with visible mechanism on top and on the viewer-facing face.
- Every machine should look like something you could bark a shin on.

The reference for the target look is `/home/shafi/rocket-engine` — read its
`js/render.js` (especially `casing()`, `busy()`, and any of `drawPrinter`,
`drawCnc`, `drawFurnace`, `drawTeststand`) before writing code. Do not copy its
subject; copy its construction discipline.

## Known open bugs — do not treat as new, and mention if you fix them

1. **`s3DownloadMs` is flat.** `src/js/model.js:76` returns a constant ~110 ms for
   every document size the UI can reach: the `Doc` slider maxes at 5120 KB and
   `S3_CHUNK_KB` is also 5120, so `possibleConc` is always 1 and `chunkKb` is
   pinned to the full 5120 rather than the actual size. A 1 KB document is charged
   the same S3 time as a 5 MB one. Fix is roughly
   `Math.min(sizeKb, S3_CHUNK_KB)` for the last chunk.
2. Because of (1), the gateway narration in `src/js/world.js` ends with *"Drag Doc
   Size up and watch the S3 download time grow in the bar chart"*, which does not
   happen. Fixing (1) makes that sentence true again.
3. The belt's animated treads and arrows had their drift direction reversed
   (`js/factory.js` — phase is now added, not subtracted). **The same bug exists
   unfixed in `/home/shafi/rocket-engine`** at `js/render.js:253` and `:267`. Do
   not "restore" EC to match rocket here.
